# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

> **Language:** English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

> This is the English version of this project's adapted code of conduct. It is adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and modified for the project's current single-maintainer reporting and enforcement process. The Chinese version is the project's primary adaptation; this English version translates it faithfully.

## Our Pledge

In order to foster an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge that participation in this project is free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that create a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward others
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best not only for us as individuals but for the community as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical address or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this code of conduct and for taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussion areas, and code communication), and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainer responsibility is held by GitHub `pomelotea-yuzu` / Gitee `yuzu-tea-duck`. For conduct complaints, check whether private messaging is available on the [Gitee maintainer profile](https://gitee.com/yuzu-tea-duck); actual reception through this channel still needs verification, and the GitHub account name must not be treated as a general private-messaging address. If contact is difficult, publicly request a private contact method without including personal information or complaint details; the relevant platform's own reporting feature is also available. Technical security vulnerabilities are handled separately under the [Security Policy](SECURITY.en.md).

Reports should include, as far as possible, where and when the incident occurred, a description of the behavior, and necessary evidence. Reporters are not required to disclose their identity or to argue directly with the person complained about. Maintainers keep and share information on a need-to-know basis and do not expose the reporter's identity in public handling notes without permission. No one may retaliate against a person for making a good-faith report.

Provide necessary evidence snippets through an available private channel; redact API keys, unrelated individuals' identities, and unauthorized business content first. Do not publish complete chats, traces, or an entire data directory to prove a point; screenshots and attachments follow the same principle.

In handling a report, maintainers first verify the facts and give the relevant parties an opportunity to explain, then take measures proportional to the severity and repetition of the behavior: requesting correction, a warning, removing violating content, limiting participation, or banning participation. In urgent cases, the spread of harmful content may be limited first and reviewed afterward. Handling should record the reasons, scope, and, where applicable, duration or recovery conditions, and notify the relevant parties through available channels; public notes keep only necessary information.

Parties may add facts or request reconsideration through the original handling channel. If a complaint involves the maintainer, the conflict of interest should be disclosed, and if a suitable non-conflicted person is available and the parties agree, that person may be invited to assist. There is currently only a single maintainer and no independent appeal body, so independent adjudication cannot be guaranteed; this limitation should be stated clearly when fair handling is not possible, and platform behavior can be referred to the platform's reporting channels.
